---

## Captions

- **Burn captions in** — 80%+ watch with sound off
- Font: **Montserrat Bold** (white text, black stroke/outline)
- Style: word-by-word highlight animation ("karaoke" style)
- Size: 60-80px at 1080p
- Position: center of screen, within middle 60% of frame
- Tools: **CapCut** (free) or **Submagic** ($14/mo for fancier animation)

---

## Audio

- Voiceover + quiet lo-fi background music at 10-20% volume
- CapCut has a free music library
- Use trending sounds for meme-style content (not tutorials)

## Posting Strategy

| Platform | Post order | Best time (EST) | Best days | Hashtags |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|-----------|----------|
| TikTok | 1st | 8 PM | Sun, Tue, Thu | 3-5: `#chesstok #chesslesson #learnchess` |
| Instagram Reels | 2nd (2-4 hrs later) | 7 AM | Wed, Thu | 5-8: add `#chessstrategy #brainteaser #chessgame` |
| YouTube Shorts | 3rd (next day) | 12 PM | Fri, Sat | 1 in title: `#Chess` |

- Post consistently at the same time daily for 30+ days
- Never cross-post with another platform's watermark — download the original file
- YouTube Shorts can go viral weeks later; don't judge early numbers

## Repeatable Content Ideas

These map directly to Chess Path features:

- **Daily Rook puzzle** → daily "Can you solve this?" video (built-in content engine)
- **Opening trees** → "Learn the Italian Game in 30 seconds" walkthrough
- **Level Tests** → "Can you pass this Level 5 test?" challenge
- **Streak celebrations** → gamification content (à la Duolingo)
- **Before/after** → "800 ELO vs 1200 ELO: the difference is ONE concept"
- **Checkmate in X** → pause-and-solve format (drives rewatches + comments)

---

## Hook Formulas (first 1.5 seconds)

Always have text on screen + say it aloud. Movement in the first frame.

- **Bold claim:** "95% of beginners lose because of this one move"
- **Direct challenge:** "Can you find the winning move? 90% of players can't"
- **Curiosity gap:** "The move every grandmaster knows that you don't..."
- **Myth buster:** "Stop playing the Queen's Gambit. Here's why."
- **Identity:** "Are you a beginner in chess?" / "Are you between 800 and 1500 ELO?"

---

## The Duolingo Playbook (steal liberally)

1. **Build a character.** The Daily Rook could become Chess Path's mascot — a rook with attitude that judges your openings.
2. **Speed over polish.** Post daily, iterate constantly. Most posts won't go viral — volume increases breakout chances.
3. **Trend-jack aggressively.** Jump on trends within hours, not days. Chess twist on trending format.
4. **Product-adjacent, not product-focused.** Make chess content that subtly features the app. Don't make ads.
5. **Track the funnel.** Add "How did you hear about us?" to onboarding to measure what's working.
