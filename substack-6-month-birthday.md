# I Built a Chess Site for People Who Don't Know How to Play Chess

*No notation. No stern grandmasters. No 600 years of theory dumped on your head. Just you, a board, and an AI named Rookie who is rooting for you way too hard.*

---

Here's the thing nobody tells you: **chess isn't hard. It's just badly taught.** Everyone hands a beginner a wall of theory and a disapproving look, and then acts surprised when normal people bounce off.

So I built the opposite. **Chess Path** is a site that teaches chess to people who have never played — the way you'd actually want to learn it. You don't read about the knight. You *move* the knight. Five minutes in, you've won something.

Let me show you the two doors in, and then get out of your way.
