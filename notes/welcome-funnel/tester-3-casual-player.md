# Tester 3 — Casual Player

**Route taken:** Explored all three options, would only click "Give me a challenge"
**Overall sentiment:** Mixed — loves the logo, frustrated by everything else
**Chess level:** Knows how to play but struggles ("extremely smart but chess is hard")

## Key Takeaways

1. **Logo is a standout.** Dual-dimension chess piece + board design, color coordination between text and image — called it "brilliant" and "striking."
2. **Tagline feels off.** "Let's get you to the right place" — who is "us"? Feels disingenuous, word salad. Disconnect from the strong logo.
3. **Options are unclear.** No explanation of time commitment, what you'll learn, or what happens when you click. "I don't know what I'm getting into."
4. **ELO is jargon.** Doesn't know what ELO means. Button doesn't explain what it will do with the rating.
5. **"Give me a challenge" is the only clear option.** "22 puzzles" tells them exactly what to expect — time, scope, commitment. The other two options lack this.
6. **Wants personal connection.** Who made this? Why? Photo of creator with chess board. "Gimme that personal connection."
7. **Navigation broken.** Couldn't figure out how to go back after clicking into an option.
8. **"Learn" text too small.** Almost missed the subtitle "Learn how the pieces move."

## Likes
- ChessPath logo design (dual-dimension chess piece imagery)
- Color coordination between logo text and chess piece image
- "Give me a challenge" option showing 22 puzzles count — sets clear expectations

## Dislikes
- "Let's get you to the right place" tagline — feels disingenuous
- Blank space below the main options
- No explanation of what ELO means
- "I don't know how to play chess" option lacks clarity on content and time commitment
- "Learn" text is too small
- No indication of time commitment for the learning option
- ELO button doesn't explain what it will do with the rating
- No info about who created the site or their background
- No photo or personal element from the creator
- Navigation confusion — couldn't figure out how to go back
