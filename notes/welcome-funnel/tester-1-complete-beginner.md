# Tester 1 — Complete Beginner

**Route taken:** "I don't know how to play chess"
**Overall sentiment:** Negative — would not continue
**Chess level:** True beginner (doesn't know piece names)

## Key Takeaways

1. **Demo teaches mechanics, not chess.** "Move this here, move that" without explaining *why* — no strategy, no context. User left feeling they learned nothing.
2. **Zero basics coverage.** No piece names, no movement rules, no objective of the game. "What is this piece called? Is this king? Is this queen? What's the point of chess?"
3. **Homepage doesn't sell.** No explanation of why to choose Chess Path or how it works. "This doesn't tell me much of why I should choose this."
4. **Interaction itself was fun.** Moving pieces around felt easy and somewhat enjoyable — the core mechanic works, the content doesn't.
5. **Demo felt too short.** Ended abruptly, didn't feel like a complete experience.

## Likes
- Simple homepage layout
- Easy, somewhat fun interaction

## Dislikes
- No explanation of moves or strategy
- No chess basics (piece names, how pieces move, objective)
- Homepage lacks value prop
- Demo too brief and uninformative
- Would not continue using the site

## Quotes
- "I don't think I learned anything from that at all"
- "If I didn't know anything about chess, I still don't know anything about chess with that demonstration"
- "I definitely wouldn't continue going further because there wasn't much there"
- "I wish it would say, move this here because this piece only moves x places"
