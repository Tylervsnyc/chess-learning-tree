# Tester 4 — Casual Player

**Route taken:** Explored options, tried puzzles
**Overall sentiment:** Negative — would give up due to confusion
**Chess level:** Casual player (plays with friends/family, can think 3-4 moves ahead, doesn't know ELO or en passant)

## Key Takeaways

1. **Likes the concept.** Quick, bite-sized puzzles appeal to them. Format is right, execution is wrong.
2. **ELO is meaningless jargon.** Doesn't know what ELO is, wouldn't know what number to enter. Needs simpler language or a quiz.
3. **No onboarding.** Clicks in and "it goes right in" — no context, no explanation, no hand-holding.
4. **Completely lost in puzzles.** "I have no idea what's going on here." Would give up because they don't know what they're supposed to do.
5. **Nothing grabs attention.** Graphics too simple, not enough depth, nothing compelling on the page.
6. **Wants a skill assessment.** Suggested quiz-style questions: "Can you think two moves ahead? Three moves ahead? How often do you play?"

## Likes
- Quick and easy format to jump into lessons
- Quick puzzle format concept — bite-sized is appealing

## Dislikes
- No explanation of what ELO means before asking for rating
- Jumps straight into content without onboarding or context
- No skill assessment quiz or simpler way to determine level
- Confusing interface — no clarity on what user should do
- Graphics are too simple
- Lack of depth in content
- Nothing compelling to grab user attention
- No explanation of what to do in puzzles — would give up
