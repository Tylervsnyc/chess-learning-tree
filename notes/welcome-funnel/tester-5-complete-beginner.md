# Tester 5 — Complete Beginner

**Route taken:** "I don't know how to play chess"
**Overall sentiment:** Mixed — enjoyed the practice, frustrated by the experience around it
**Chess level:** True beginner

## Key Takeaways

1. **Practice itself is fun.** Enjoyed the interactive chess practice and learning basic moves. Core mechanic works.
2. **Felt ambushed into a lesson.** Clicked something and "things just started happening" — wants to be prepped before a lesson begins.
3. **Felt trapped.** No back button, no way to navigate away from the lesson. Described it as a "lock screen" that traps you.
4. **Wants more transparency.** No reviews on the site, no info about data collection, not enough information on the page.
5. **Instructions unclear.** Didn't understand what they were being asked to do at first. Wants more straightforward guidance before starting.

## Likes
- Interactive chess practice teaching basic moves
- The practice experience itself was fun
- Lesson content seemed interesting after completing it

## Dislikes
- Lesson started automatically without warning after clicking
- No reviews visible on the website
- No ability to go back or navigate away from the lesson
- Lack of information on the page
- Feeling trapped in a lock screen after starting
- No transparency about data collection
- Instructions not clear about what user was being asked to do
