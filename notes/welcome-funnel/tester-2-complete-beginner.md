# Tester 2 — Complete Beginner

**Route taken:** "I don't know how to play chess"
**Overall sentiment:** Very positive — would use the app and come back
**Chess level:** True beginner (has tried other programs, never stuck)

## Key Takeaways

1. **Piece movement tutorial works.** Sub-minute tutorial taught all piece movements. User felt confident they'd remember after a few reps. Same page as Tester 1, completely different reaction.
2. **Fun factor is the hook.** "That was fun" repeated multiple times. The interaction itself — sounds, feel, colors — made them want to keep going.
3. **Immediate onboarding wins.** Loved jumping straight into "what's your level?" without lengthy setup. No friction.
4. **Superior to competitors.** Has tried similar programs before but "they just don't look or feel as good" and gets bored quickly. Chess Path held attention.
5. **Look and feel is a differentiator.** Vibrant colors, satisfying sounds, tactile piece movement all called out specifically.
