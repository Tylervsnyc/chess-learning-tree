**Date:** 2026-05-13
**Method:** each ability force-seeded at T3 for every level of a 10-level run. Bot = T3 Casual. 8 trials per run. Compared to a no-ability baseline.

## Headlines

1. **Detonate is the universal winner.** Solo-completes all 7 hard runs at T3. Average +4.50 levels gained. The 3×3 blast clears pawn walls, cracks defended chains, and removes multiple high-value pieces in one cast.

2. **Aegis is the universal #2.** Solo-completes 5 of 7 runs. Defensive timing is dominant because most run-ending events are unexpected captures.

3. **Phase Step is the best "movement crutch."** Solo-completes 3 of 7 — particularly strong where choke points exist (Royal Court, Hazard Maze, Boss Gauntlet).

4. **Freeze Ray and Knight Hop are NET NEGATIVE.** Bots use them and lose levels they would have completed without. Burn turns freezing wrong targets / jumping into worse positions.

5. **Queenkiller is below expectations.** Top finding at Royal Court (+4.50) but average lift is only +1.07. The targeted nature constrains its usefulness when bots can route around queens.

## Recommendations for real-player release

1. **Ship Detonate to real players first.** It's the most universally valuable ability. Predict measurable real-player improvement.
2. **Ship Aegis if not already shipped at high quality.** Same data.
3. **Rebalance Freeze Ray and Knight Hop** — both are bot-detrimental. Either redesign or downgrade their offer probability.
4. **Queenkiller needs more iteration** — the design is right (queens are killers) but the bot can't capitalize. Either improve bot eval or change Queenkiller's mechanic (e.g., remove all queens within radius, not just one).

## Methodology caveats

- Bots are T3 (1-ply principled). T4/T5 results on The Gauntlet show similar rankings but lower magnitudes — because T4/T5 baselines are already higher.
- 8 trials per cell is light. Run with 25+ for production-quality precision.
- Each ability is locked at T3 for the whole run. Real players acquire at T1 and upgrade — this measures the "T3 ceiling," not the player experience curve.
- "fixed-t3" mode only — "upgrading" mode (T1→T5 across the run) reveals weaker results because bots die before higher tiers unlock.
