## Headlines

1. **Mirror is the breakthrough.** Ties Detonate at #1 with +4.66 average Δ and 7/7 runs solo-completed. A defensive "next attacker dies" mechanic that the bots can use without setup — same shape as Aegis but stronger because it kills the attacker outright.

2. **Tempo Vault is a sleeper hit.** +4.26 average, 4/7 runs solo-completed. Vaporizes any enemy for tempo (no material capture value to the bot — just pure tempo gain), which triggers offers more often, which compounds across the run. Validates Tyler's stop-short-for-tempo principle as a winning meta-strategy.

3. **6 of the top 10 are NEW abilities.** Mirror, Tempo Vault, Bait, Quickstep, Slayer, Decoy. The new candidates outperformed the original pool — 4 NEW abilities in the top 8.

4. **Defensive abilities dominate the top.** #1 Detonate (offense), #2 Mirror (defense), #3 Tempo Vault (utility), #4 Aegis (defense), #5 Bait (defense). The bot's biggest unmet need is staying alive.

5. **Many novel mechanics flopped.** Magnet, Pushback (move enemies around) and Rally (instant tempo) all net negative. The pattern matches earlier findings: bots can't capitalize on **board-control** mechanics that don't directly remove threats or grant moves. Foresight (auto-shield) also net negative — the bots already raise shields reactively, so auto-shield wastes charges.

## Top recommendations for real-player release

Promote in order, in this order:

1. **Detonate** — already in the game, top performer
2. **Mirror** — NEW. Defensive auto-counter. Strongest new candidate.
3. **Aegis** — already in the game
4. **Tempo Vault** — NEW. Validates the meta-strategy of capturing for tempo, not material.
5. **Bait** — NEW. Strong tactical defense.

The bottom 5 NEW (Magnet, Pushback, Rally, Bedrock, Recall) should be **retired** before any real-player exposure — they reduce win rates.

## Caveats

- 5 trials/cell is light. 25+ would tighten the rankings.
- All measurements at T3 fixed across the run. Real players acquire at T1 and upgrade — actual player-game impact will differ.
- Several "duds" might shine at T5 (e.g., Beeline T5 wins outright if file is clear) — we measured T3 only.
- The non-determinism in pawn-ai.ts adds ~5pp noise. Rankings near the middle are less reliable than the top/bottom 5.
