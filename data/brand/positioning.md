# The Chess Path — Brand Positioning

The source of truth for what we are, who we're for, and how we're different.
Landing copy, emails, IG, and Rookie's voice all pull from this doc. If a piece
of copy doesn't ladder up to something here, it's off-brand.

---

## One line

**The chess app that's rooting for you.**

Alternates (same idea, different angle):
- *Learn chess with a friend who's figuring it out too.*
- *Chess, minus the part where you feel dumb.*

## The promise

Go from "I don't know how the pieces move" to "I just won my first checkmate"
in under 5 minutes — and want to come back tomorrow. You're never lost, never
judged, and never alone: Rookie is right there with you.

---

## Who it's for

Complete beginners. On their phone. Curious about chess but quietly afraid of
it — they think it's for geniuses and they'll look stupid. They are NOT chess
players yet. They don't want a tool. They want a way in that doesn't make them
feel dumb.

**What they feel before us:** "Chess is for smart people. I'd embarrass myself."
**What they feel after the first win:** "Wait — I can do this. And that was fun."

---

## The competitors (and why we're not them)

### vs Chess.com / Lichess — *the gym*
Endless equipment, assumes you already know how to lift. Built for people who
already love chess and want depth. For a beginner it's a wall of ratings,
notation, and strangers who crush you. **Chess.com makes you feel dumb.**
We don't out-feature them — we'd lose. We win on the *first hour*, the part they
treat as table stakes and we treat as the whole product.

### vs Duolingo — *the loop, perfected, but for languages*
Duolingo nailed the learning loop: bite-sized, streaks, a mascot that pulls you
back. We borrow that loop shamelessly. But the owl **nags** — it's a guilt
machine. And it teaches languages, not chess. **Duolingo makes you feel
nagged.** We keep the loop; we throw out the guilt.

### What only we have — *Rookie*
Neither competitor has a character you'd call a friend. Rookie is an AI who is
**learning to feel emotions while you learn chess.** You're not being taught by a
bored expert — you're learning *alongside* someone as new to feelings as you are
to chess, who's genuinely delighted when you get it right. That's the moat.
Anyone can copy "easier chess." Nobody can copy Rookie.

| | Makes you feel | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Chess.com | dumb | a tool for players |
| Duolingo | nagged | a loop for languages |
| **The Chess Path** | **accompanied · liked** | **a companion who's learning too** |

## The three pillars

Everything we build should serve at least one. The best things serve all three.

1. **Never feel dumb.** Gentle pacing, instant clear feedback, wrong moves are
   funny not punishing. You always know what to do next.
2. **Quick wins, real momentum.** A win in the first 5 minutes. Always
   progressing, always one tap from the next thing. The tutorial IS the pitch.
3. **Rookie's with you.** Personality on every screen. Reacting, learning,
   growing alongside you. The reason you come back isn't a streak counter — it's
   that someone's happy to see you.

---

## Voice in one breath

Fun, friendly, encouraging — through Rookie, an earnest unsupervised AI
discovering feelings (Wheatley energy). The humor is the gap between knowing
everything about chess and nothing about feelings. Never sarcastic, never cruel,
never corporate. Full rules: `.claude/rookie-voice-bible.md`.

---

## What we are NOT (anti-brand)

- NOT Chess.com/Lichess — dense, assumes knowledge, intimidating.
- NOT corporate SaaS — no generic gradients, stock photos, "Get Started Free."
- NOT a kids' app — playful, but adults use this. Never cartoonish or patronizing.
- NOT a nag — we pull you back with delight, not guilt.

---

## How to use this doc

- **Writing landing copy?** Lead with a pillar (usually #2: quick win) and let
  Rookie's voice carry it. Don't pitch chess — start the win.
- **Writing an email?** It's from Rookie. Pillar #3. No emojis, ever.
- **Naming a feature / writing a headline?** If it could appear on Chess.com's
  site unchanged, it's wrong.
