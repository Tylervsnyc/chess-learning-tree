# Openings Curriculum Plan

## First 5 Openings

| # | Opening | Side | Status | Why |
|---|---------|------|--------|-----|
| 1 | **Italian Game** | White | Planned | Most natural "develop and attack" — teaches center control, hooks beginners with Fried Liver traps |
| 2 | **Pirc Defense** | Black | Planned | One setup (d6/Nf6/g6/Bg7) works against virtually any 1.e4 — no memorizing separate defenses |
| 3 | **Ruy Lopez** | White | Built (Level 1) | Deepens 1.e4 e5 understanding with slower strategic ideas after Italian teaches aggression |
| 4 | **Sicilian Defense** | Black | Planned | #1 response to 1.e4 online — beginners face it constantly and need the asymmetric pawn structure basics |
| 5 | **London System** | White | Planned | One-system answer to 1.d4 — Bf4/e3/Nf3/Bd3 works against everything, reliable White d4 repertoire |

## Openings 6-10

| # | Opening | Side | Status | Why |
|---|---------|------|--------|-----|
| 6 | **Queen's Gambit Declined** | Black | Planned | Most common thing after 1.d4 — teaches holding center with d5/e6 |
| 7 | **Scotch Game** | White | Planned | Faster alternative to Ruy/Italian — immediate center opening, gives variety |
| 8 | **Caro-Kann** | Black | Planned | Solid, beginner-proof 1.e4 defense — bishop gets out to f5 early, forgiving structure |
| 9 | **King's Indian Defense** | Black | Planned | Aggressive 1.d4 system — fianchetto + pawn storms, complements the solid QGD |
| 10 | **King's Gambit** | White | Planned | Romantic chess as a reward — by now they understand enough to survive the pawn sacrifice |

## Ordering Logic

- **1.e4 world first** (slots 1-4) since 80%+ of beginner games start there, then **1.d4 gateway** at slot 5
- **White-Black alternation** so learners always have both colors covered
- **One-system openings early** (Pirc, London) — learn one setup, play it against everything. More branching theory comes later

## Notable Omissions

- **French Defense** — bad bishop on c8 frustrates beginners. Caro-Kann covers the same "solid Black vs 1.e4" niche with fewer structural headaches. Could be slot 11.
