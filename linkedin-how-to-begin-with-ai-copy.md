## The post

Save this post for next time you begin an AI project.

It's the exact exercise I run with every client in our first session — I'm giving it away free. It's how you start thinking in AI, and it's not scary, I promise.

Set aside 30 minutes and play the five moves:

1. Pick one boring task. Something you already do every week. Not a new idea — the boring one.

2. Gather your inputs. The emails, files, and notes that task uses. You already have all of them.

3. Show 3 examples of it done well. Show it, don't describe it. This is the move everyone skips, and it's the whole trick.

4. Write the steps in plain words, like you're training a new hire.

5. Run it. Fix one thing. Repeat. Three rounds is usually enough.

That's the whole exercise. No platform, no "AI strategy," nothing to buy — it works on anything.

If you get stuck on a step, tell me which one in the comments and I'll help — that's free too.
