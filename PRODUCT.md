# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Complete beginners to chess who want to learn without feeling dumb. On their phones, curious but not committed. Job to be done: go from "I don't know how the pieces move" to "I just solved my first checkmate" in under 5 minutes — and want to come back tomorrow. Chess Boxing users add a second context: real chess-boxing athletes and gym-goers (NYC/Gleason's cohort) using the app as a training tool and competition ladder.

## Product Purpose

The Chess Path (chesspath.app) — Duolingo for chess. Daily loop = Play + Learn + Chess Boxing workout, with a do-anything streak as the spine. Success = engaged DAU (users who come back and do something). Chess Boxing is the iOS-app pivot: rounds of puzzles and punches, leaderboards, global competition for the real sport.

## Brand Personality

Fun, Friendly, Encouraging — with a twist: Rookie, an AI rook who is unreasonably, specifically invested in your chess. Proud and brief when you win; a dramatic sore loser when she loses. Playful game energy, never cruel, never corporate. No emojis anywhere.

## Anti-references

- NOT Chess.com/Lichess: dense, assumes knowledge, intimidating chess-club energy.
- NOT corporate SaaS: no generic gradients, stock photos, "Get Started Free" energy.
- NOT a kids' app: playful, but adults use this — never cartoonish or patronizing.

## Design Principles

1. Rookie's on your side — every screen feels like she's there, over-invested in you.
2. Show, don't explain — interactive beats text; quips do the heavy lifting.
3. Celebrate everything, even failure — mistakes are funny, not punishing.
4. Momentum over perfection — always a visible next step; never trap someone.
5. Earn the signup — delight first, account creation after the first "I did it!"

Visual language: light, clean, Duolingo-inspired; chunky flat-color buttons with hard 3-4px bottom shadows that press flat (no gradients); DM Sans everywhere; tokens + rules in `.claude/design-system.md` and RULES.md §43/§50. Chess Boxing surfaces run a navy gym palette (#1b2a4a family) with the red hanging sign as the anchor.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first (360px up), tap targets ≥44px, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives on all animation, body text ≥4.5:1 contrast. Complete beginners are the audience — copy must never assume chess knowledge.
