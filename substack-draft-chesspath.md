# 25% of Duolingo's Chess Puzzles Don't Make Sense, So I Made Chess Path

I've been teaching chess for over 20 years. I was the head teacher at the Village Chess Shop back in 2009. I founded Storytime Chess to help kids fall in love with the game. Teaching chess in a clear, understandable way isn't just something I do. It's something I care deeply about.

I taught chess in a preschool for over 10 years. I've seen 3, 4, and 5-year-olds legitimately play and experience joy from the game of chess. So when people say chess is only for nerds or rocket scientists or "smart people," I know that's not true. I've watched little kids prove it wrong every single day. That's why seeing chess taught in a confusing way really irks me.

## I Don't Trust Duolingo

When Duolingo launched their chess course, I was genuinely excited. Finally, a major player bringing chess education to the masses.

Then I actually went through it.

I worked through Section 7, their most recent section, and marked whether each puzzle made sense. If it didn't make sense, I wrote down the reason. Where was their education system breaking down? What was confusing the learner?

After a while, there were so many puzzles that didn't make sense. It became hard to tell if a puzzle was difficult or just confusing.

That's a huge problem. You need to trust the material you're trying to learn.

When I was going through these puzzles, I lost that trust. And if I lost it as a 1900-rated player who's been teaching for two decades, imagine what happens to a beginner.

## 885 Puzzles, Reviewed By Hand

I went through all 885 puzzles in Section 7 and categorized every single one.

The results:

- **672 puzzles (76%)** were effective. Good stuff. No complaints.
- **178 puzzles (20%)** didn't work.
- **35 puzzles (4%)** were borderline.

That's roughly **1 in 4 puzzles** that will actively confuse a beginner trying to learn.

## What "Doesn't Work" Actually Means

**Theme Not Present (40% of issues):** The puzzle says "practice forks" but the solution doesn't use a fork. Your brain practices the wrong pattern. You think you don't understand forks. You actually don't understand forks now.

**Branching Variations (32%):** The opponent has multiple reasonable responses, creating a calculation tree that overwhelms anyone under 1200. These puzzles teach advanced calculation, not the stated concept.

**Opaque Payoff (12%):** You complete the sequence and you're slightly better? No checkmate, no piece captured. Just a vague positional advantage that takes a 2000-rated player to appreciate.

**Weird AI Positions (5%):** These positions would never happen in a real game. They feel fake because they are. Generated by an algorithm that doesn't understand how humans play.

## How I Built Chess Path

I didn't just complain. I built what I wished existed.

I started with a database of over **5 million chess puzzles** that have been solved thousands of times by real people. Not AI-generated positions. Real games, real patterns.

Then I separated them by rating (ELO) and by tactical theme. And here's where 20 years of teaching comes in: I used learning science to order the puzzles in a way that would be **extremely satisfying** to go through.

The goal wasn't just "teach chess tactics." The goal was to give someone that incredible feeling of learning a new skill. Of having competence in something they didn't even know about before.

Every puzzle has been hand-checked. If it says "fork practice," there's a fork. If it says "800 ELO," an 800-rated player can solve it.

You can trust it.

## What Chess Path Looks Like

The curriculum starts with **"Level 1: How to Lose Friends"** (400-800 ELO):
- Checkmates first (queen & rook, classic patterns, multi-move)
- Then "Take Their Stuff" (forks, pins, skewers)
- Then endgames
- Reviews after each section

Then **"Level 2: The Assassin's Toolkit"** (800-1000 ELO) goes deeper.

There's also [The Daily Rook](https://chesspath.app/daily-challenge): 5 minutes on the clock, puzzles get harder as you go, 3 mistakes and you're out. Same puzzles for everyone, so you can compete with friends.

**Free forever.** Premium members help keep it that way.
