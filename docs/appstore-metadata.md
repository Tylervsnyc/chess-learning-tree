## Description

Chess boxing is a real sport: alternating rounds of chess and boxing until someone gets checkmated or knocked out. Chess Boxing brings the training version to your phone. No opponent, no gloves required — just a board, a timer, and a fight bell.

TWO WAYS TO TRAIN

Puzzle Boxing
Timed rounds of chess puzzles alternate with timed rounds of boxing exercise — shadowboxing, jump rope, whatever gets your heart rate up. Solve as many as you can while you're fresh, then try again gassed. That's the whole sport in one session.

Play Boxing
One full game against Rookie, our resident chess AI, split across chess rounds. When the bell rings, the board freezes and you box. When it rings again, you come back to the same position with your heart still pounding. Judges' cards score chess and boxing separately, then add it up.

WHY IT WORKS

Chess trains focus. Boxing trains conditioning. Doing them back to back trains the thing chess players and fighters both need most: thinking clearly when you're tired.

BUILT FOR SHOWING UP

- Streaks: finish one session a day and the streak counts.
- Leaderboards: ranked puzzle boxing, plus private crews so you can compete with friends or your gym.
- Rookie in your corner: an over-invested AI coach who cheers when you win and melts down when she loses.
- Levels for everyone: puzzles start easy and climb with you.

Chess Boxing is free. Sign in with Apple, Google, or email to save your streak and climb the leaderboard, or just open the app and start swinging.
