# 2026 Candidates — Rules

Rules for the Candidates tournament content pages and video assets.

---

## Title Cards

Title cards are 9:16 (540x960) video thumbnails/intro frames for round recap videos.

### Photos
- **You must see the player's entire face in the headshot.** No cropping foreheads, chins, or sides of faces.
- Use `object-contain` or careful `object-position` to ensure the full face is always visible — never blindly use `object-cover object-top`.
- If a photo doesn't fit well at the required size, scale down or add padding rather than cropping the face.

### Content
- Title: "Re-Cap in 59 Seconds" (always this exact copy)
- Show: Round number, both player names, ratings, color assignments, country flags
- Branding: chesspath.app logo + text must appear

### Style
- Lighter background — not pure black, use a warm dark tone (e.g. `#1a2228` range)
- Player photos should dominate the card
- Lightning bolt diagonal divider separating the two players
- BreathingRook logo at the bolt's center point
- Dramatic but readable — this is a YouTube thumbnail, needs to pop at small sizes
